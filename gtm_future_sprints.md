# Future GTM Sprints

These sprints come **after** the MVP and are not completed work. All targets are proposals to validate.

**Current MVP:** An n8n workflow that turns a job posting and a candidate's background into a one-page interview preparation report.

**Value proposition:** In five minutes, a job seeker knows what to say about the company, which of their examples to use, what to prepare, and which questions to expect.

**Go-to-market path:** Prove value with individual job seekers, win an institutional pilot, then test whether organizations will pay.

## Summary

| | Sprint 1: Prove usefulness | Sprint 2: Institutional pilot | Sprint 3: Willingness to pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | 3 weeks | 4 weeks | 4 weeks |
| **Audience** | Active job seekers | University career centers | Bootcamps and career coaches |
| **Channel or motion** | LinkedIn and alumni groups | Direct outreach | Partner referrals |
| **Key deliverable** | Hosted beta and feedback survey | Pilot offer and demo pack | Pricing and per-report plan |
| **Success metric** | 20 reports, 70% rated useful | 1 agreed pilot | 5 paying partners |

## Sprint 1: Prove usefulness with job seekers

- **Goal:** Confirm that real job seekers find the report useful before a real interview.
- **Target user:** People with an interview scheduled in the next two weeks.
- **Channel or motion:** Personal LinkedIn posts and direct invitations in bootcamp and alumni groups.
- **Key deliverable:** A hosted beta with a simple form, a privacy notice, and a short survey sent after each report.
- **Success metric:** 20 reports generated for real interviews, with at least 70% rated useful by their users.
- **Decision:** If usefulness is below 70%, improve the weakest report sections and repeat the test before starting Sprint 2.

## Sprint 2: Win a first institutional pilot

- **Goal:** Test whether career services teams would offer the report to the people they support.
- **Target buyer:** Heads of university career centers. Career advisers are the day-to-day users.
- **Channel or motion:** Direct outreach to career centers, starting with universities we already have a connection to, followed by a live demo.
- **Key deliverable:** A one-page pilot offer, a demo pack with sample reports and Sprint 1 results, and a data-protection summary.
- **Success metric:** 1 pilot agreed with a career center.
- **Decision:** If no center agrees after outreach, use the objections we hear to revise the offer or move directly to the Sprint 3 audience.

## Sprint 3: Test willingness to pay

- **Goal:** Find out whether organizations that prepare people for interviews will pay for reports.
- **Target buyer:** Bootcamp career teams and independent career coaches.
- **Channel or motion:** Referrals from pilot contacts, alumni, and career-coach networks.
- **Key deliverable:** A simple pricing page, a per-report plan, and a cost model that includes API usage.
- **Success metric:** 5 paying partners, each buying at least one report package.
- **Decision:** If fewer than 5 partners pay, compare the objections on price and value, then decide whether to change the buyer or the pricing.

## What must be true before real users

| Need | Why it matters | Sprint |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy notice and data-deletion process | CVs contain personal data | 1 |
| Stable hosting and a usable form | The current MVP is not a production service | 1 |
| Review of each API's terms | Free plans may restrict commercial use | 1 and 3 |
| Paid API plans where required | Sprint 3 is commercial use | 3 |

Multi-tenant accounts, production authentication, and paid advertising remain out of scope for the MVP.
